Chapter One

Why this exists

This page didn't start as a business. It started because I kept getting the same messages, again and again. "Do you have notes for this?" "I don't understand this topic." "I have NORCET next month and I'm panicking."

Every field we cover, Nursing, GNM, ANM, B.Pharma, OT, Lab Tech, leads to the same place. Someone's bedside, someone's medication, someone's sample, someone's operation. These courses don't just test what you've memorised. They decide who's ready when it actually matters. That felt like something worth taking seriously.

So I started writing the notes I wished someone had written for me. Clear, visual, no unnecessary jargon, for whichever course you're in.

Somewhere along the way, thousands of you started reading them, from every corner of allied health, not just one. This still isn't a note selling page to me. It's a community of people from different courses, all trying to become genuinely ready for the job, and helping each other get there, one topic at a time.
